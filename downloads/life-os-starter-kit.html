The 5-pillar productivity framework

  1. Clarity — know your 3 priorities before the day starts. No clarity, no focus.
  2. Energy — schedule hard work when you're sharpest (chronotype-aware). Protect sleep.
  3. Focus — single-task deep blocks. Notifications off. Phone out of reach.
  4. Systems — recurring decisions become templates: meals, workouts, weekly review.
  5. Review — weekly review closes the loop: what worked, what to change, next week's plan.

Weekly review checklist

  • ☐ Wins — 3 things that went well (write them down, no skipping)
  • ☐ Misses — what fell off, and why (no blame, just data)
  • ☐ Inbox zero — clear email + task inbox
  • ☐ Calendar look-ahead — next week's time blocks
  • ☐ Top 3 for next week — written and scheduled
  • ☐ System tweak — one small improvement to your setup

Setup in 20 minutes

  1. Copy the dashboard table into Notion/Excel (or plain paper — same logic)
  2. Define your 5 habits (start tiny: 2-min versions)
  3. Block tomorrow: 2 deep-work slots + 1 review slot
  4. Set a recurring Sunday 30-min weekly review

The goal isn't a perfect system — it's a system that survives contact with real life. Iterate weekly.
