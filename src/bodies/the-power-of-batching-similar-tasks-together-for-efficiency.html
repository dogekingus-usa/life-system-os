Understanding Batching: What It Is and Why It Matters

Batching is a powerful strategy that involves grouping similar tasks together, whether they be at work or in your personal life. By doing so, you can save time, reduce mental fatigue, and increase overall efficiency.

For example, instead of switching between emails and phone calls throughout the day, batching allows you to dedicate specific blocks of time to each activity. This approach can significantly streamline your workflow and free up more time for high-value tasks that require focused attention.
